## About Me
I recently received my Ph.D. in **Macroecology** from the **Wuhan Botanical Garden, Chinese Academy of Sciences**.

I received my Research Master’s degree in **Ecology, Evolution & Conservation** from **Imperial College London**, and my Bachelor’s degree in **Ecology** from **Sun Yat-Sen University**.

My research focuses on the macroecology and biogeography of multi-trophic biotic interactions. By integrating species traits, distributions, environmental factors and interaction data, I investigate the large-scale patterns and driving mechanisms of biotic interactions. More broadly, I aim to reveal how biotic interactions shape the assembly, maintenance, and dynamics of biodiversity under global environmental change.
