I built a small web-based tool designed to generate short meditation-style prompts for focused work.

The tool is intended for moments when starting or maintaining concentration is difficult.  
Rather than providing long guided sessions, it produces concise textual prompts that can be read in under a minute and used as a mental anchor before entering a focused work period.

This approach is particularly useful for writing, data analysis, and other cognitively demanding tasks where a brief reset is more practical than extended meditation.

**Tool link:**  
[https://xsm4n9qhd8.coze.site/](https://xsm4n9qhd8.coze.site/)

## How it can be used

- As a short pre-work ritual to establish focus
- During breaks to reset attention without leaving the task context
- As a lightweight alternative to audio-guided meditation

## Notes

The tool is intentionally minimal and text-based, prioritizing clarity and low cognitive load over immersive features.
